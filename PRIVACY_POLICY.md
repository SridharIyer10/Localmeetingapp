# Privacy

Intake Pro runs on your computer.

- **Audio and transcripts** are processed and stored locally, in the app's data folder.
- **No analytics or telemetry.** The app does not send usage data anywhere.
- **No accounts, no cloud transcription, no audio upload.**
- **Summaries** are generated by the model you configure. With a local model (built-in or
  Ollama) nothing leaves your machine. If you configure a cloud provider, the transcript
  text for the meeting you summarise is sent to that provider under its terms, and the API
  key you enter is stored on your computer.
- **Downloads.** The app downloads model files when you ask it to.

You can delete a meeting at any time from the sidebar; deleting it removes its transcript,
summary and recording from your computer.
